why this open-ended?

  • realistic
    • The goal is [to] prepare them for reality.
  • learning experience
    • We give them two lectures on Scrum with the same content. The first Scrum-lecture we tell them how it all works. The second Scrum-lecture happens after the first sprint. And we give them the exact same information again, just expressed differently. And I often got the comment: 'Why didn't you tell us earlier?' I did, but now you know. So show, don't tell. That is everything here.
  • guidance
  • legacy
    • This was the concept of the course from the beginning.

assessment experience

  • differentiation in collaboration
  • consistency
  • time intensive
    • You can do one team per day, so it takes you two weeks full-time to do all. [...] And the week before you're reading all those peer reviews, so you have a month where you basically don't do anything else. [...] You want to take everything into account, and you can only do that at the end. That is the big problem.
  • product vs process
    • This really is the most important aspect of the course: you always assess the process. A good process leads to a good product anyway.
  • feedback
  • works well
  • justification
    • Most of the time it results in the grade we intuitively feel, but we see why and we can explain it too.
