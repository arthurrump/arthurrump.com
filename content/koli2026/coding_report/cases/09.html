assessment experience

  • product vs process
    • The aim of the project is not for them to deliver something to a client. It's more for them to practice with database APIs, web programming, [...] and for them to learn Agile and Scrum.
  • no matching level
    • We have that section with 0, 0.4, 0.7 and 1. We collectively agreed last year that those are discrete measurements. So a 0.6 does not exist. And that often makes it hard, especially [...] if they meet all of them or most of them or the most important, in which box do you go?
