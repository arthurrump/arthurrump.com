why this open-ended?

  • realistic
  • fun
  • fraud resistance
    • What I'm really afraid of with giving too much structure and templates, especially nowadays, is that you just put it into ChatGPT and then you say 'do it' and then the more you give the structure, the more it's like accurate.
  • flexibility for students
    • [...] to allow the students to grow in different directions. So it gives you this customizability, right? Like a student maybe wants to learn more about, I don't know, game physics. [...] Then you allow them to, with the bonuses, you allow them to do a new concept, create it from scratch. And then you have another student that just wants to do the typical power up, they don't care so much about this aspect. Then again they have the freedom.
  • learning experience
  • showing genuine learning
    • The more you make it critical and open, the more you learn about genuinely [...] what the student learned.
  • guidance
    • I feel like it's a good strategy for a first year course, first study period, because imagine now if I did completely open, students would be completely lost, right?
  • easier checks
    • That's where it [openness] collides with having structure to check things, right? To do unit testing or ...
